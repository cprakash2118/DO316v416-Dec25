Problem statement:-
OpenShift Virtulization provides a native way to export a Virtual Machine (VM) + it's disks (all 
disks associated with VMs) so that:-
 - Move a VM from openshift cluster to other OCP cluster.
 - Deplicate a VM into another environment (Prod to Stage/Dev/Test etc).
 - Backup VM manifest and disk artifacts for recovery.
